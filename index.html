How do I move around?

Use A / D or the arrow keys, or tap the path. Hold Shift to walk faster. Move close to a person or object, then press E or tap its name.

Do I need a microphone?

No. Every conversation works by typing. If you choose to speak, Gnani handles your voice and the characters’ spoken replies. Subtitles stay available.

How does time travel work?

Find the pocket instrument, then turn its dial with Q or tap it on screen. You stay in the same place, thirty years apart. Look for what has changed.

What if I lose the thread?

Your journal keeps the evidence you have discovered, your current question, and a guide to the station. Open it with J. A hint is there if you need one.

Can I return later?

When saves are available, choose Continue saved visit in the same browser. Begin a new story starts with a fresh arrival and an empty journal.

Is this the complete game?

This is a playable Gnani hackathon demo with a complete case. Animation, voice pronunciation, and pacing are still being refined.
