# KAL: The Last Train

KAL is a voice-led browser mystery set at a fictional railway station in the Western Ghats. Anay arrives in 2028 to catalogue a closed station before renovation. The old report blames a signalman named Prakash for diverting the last passenger train. A brass dial takes Anay to the same station on the evening of that service in 1998. The people who remember the night are still there.

You can ask witnesses your own questions in Hindi, English or Hinglish. A witness can recognise the document you’re showing them, explain what they remember and respond to a follow-up. Testimony becomes evidence you can compare with the railway’s records. To finish the case, you have to act on what you learned in 1998 and see what survives in 2028.

Gnani’s Prisma transcribes the player’s speech and Timbre voices the witnesses. You can check and edit the transcript before sending it. Supabase holds the case facts and saved progress. The game filters the facts by witness and evidence before sending them to the dialogue model, and its clue rules determine when a discovery can advance the investigation. You can also play the complete case by typing.

For a quick demo, visit retired Jadhav at the outpost. Read the closure report and duty log, ask him about Prakash, then show him the log and ask who brought the message. Walk back to the platform and turn the dial to see the same station thirty years earlier.
